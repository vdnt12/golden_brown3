RENTAL AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC -- AMBIGUITY FIXTURE)

1. Parties
This agreement is between the owner and the occupier of House No. 21, Vidya Nagar,
Hubballi.

2. NO DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 40,000/- before occupation.

3. Rent
The Tenant shall pay rent at the market rate, to be revised from time to time as mutually
agreed between the parties.

4. Notice
Either party may terminate the tenancy by giving reasonable notice in writing.

5. Maintenance
The Tenant shall bear usual maintenance charges. Major repairs shall be carried out as
and when required by the Landlord.

6. Deductions
The Landlord may make appropriate deductions from the deposit for any damage.

7. Guests
Guests may stay for a reasonable period. Long-staying guests shall be informed to the
Landlord.

8. Subletting
Subletting is subject to clause 17.

9. Handover
The property shall be handed over in good condition, subject to normal wear and tear.
