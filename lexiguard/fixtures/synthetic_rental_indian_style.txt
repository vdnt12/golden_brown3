LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This Leave and Licence Agreement is executed on this 18th day of October 2026 at Hubballi
between Shri Ramesh Kulkarni, hereinafter called the "Licensor" / "Lessor", and Smt.
Ananya Deshpande, hereinafter called the "Licensee" / "Lessee".

WHEREAS the Licensor is the owner of premises bearing No. 7, Sai Nagar, Hubballi.

NOW THEREFORE the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM
The licence is granted for a period of 11 months commencing on 01/11/2026 and ending on
30/09/2027. The licence fee is Rs. 18,000/- per month, payable on or before the 7th of
each month.

2. DEPOSIT
The Licensee has paid an interest-free security advance of Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees One
Lakh only). The advance shall be refunded within 45 days of vacating the premises on
production of the original receipt and after permissible deductions.

3. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION
Stamp duty and registration charges, if applicable, shall be borne by the Licensee.
The agreement is executed on stamp paper of Rs. 500/-.

4. LOCK-IN
There is a lock-in period of six (6) months from 01/11/2026. If the Licensee vacates
before the lock-in period, the Licensee shall pay compensation equivalent to two months'
licence fee.

5. NOTICE
The Licensee shall give 60 days' written notice to quit. The Licensor shall give 60
days' notice, save in the case of default in payment.

6. MAINTENANCE
Routine maintenance, society charges, electricity and water charges are payable by the
Licensee. Major structural repairs are the responsibility of the Licensor.

7. PROHIBITED USES
The premises shall be used only for residential purposes. Subletting or assignment is
not permitted without written consent.

8. ANNUAL ESCALATION
The licence fee shall increase by 5% after every twelve months of occupation.

9. HANDOVER
An inventory of furniture and fixtures is annexed as Schedule I. Keys shall be returned
at the time of handover.

10. JURISDICTION
Any dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Hubballi.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement.
