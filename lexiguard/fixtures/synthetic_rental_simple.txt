RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

1. Parties
This Rental Agreement is made on 1 November 2026 between Mr. Arjun Mehta, residing at 12
Market Road, Hubballi ("Landlord"), and Ms. Priya Nair, residing at 44 Station Road,
Dharwad ("Tenant").

2. Property
The Landlord rents to the Tenant residential flat 4B, Lake View Apartments, Gokul Road,
Hubballi, together with one covered parking space.

3. Term
The tenancy begins on 1 December 2026 and ends on 31 October 2027, a term of eleven
months. The tenancy may be renewed by written agreement of both parties.

4. Rent
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 25,000/- on or before the 5th day of each
English calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account stated in Annexure A.

5. Security deposit
The Tenant has paid a security deposit of Rs. 50,000/-. The Landlord shall refund the
deposit within thirty (30) days after handover, after deducting unpaid rent and the cost
of repairs for damage beyond normal wear and tear.

6. Notice and termination
The Tenant shall give sixty (60) days' written notice before terminating the tenancy.
The Landlord shall give sixty (60) days' written notice, except where the tenancy is
terminated for non-payment of rent.

7. Maintenance and utilities
The Tenant shall pay monthly maintenance charges and electricity and water charges.
The Landlord shall carry out structural repairs.

8. Restrictions
The Tenant shall not sublet the premises or any part of it without the prior written
consent of the Landlord. No pets are permitted without written consent.

9. Late payment
If rent remains unpaid for more than ten (10) days after the due date, interest at 1%
per month on the unpaid amount applies.

10. Handover
The Tenant shall hand over the premises in the condition recorded in the inventory list
at Annexure A, with all keys.

11. Disputes
The courts at Hubballi shall have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from this
agreement.

Signed by the parties on the date first written above.
